1The Substrate — what a bet IS

Every market event — order, ack, fill, settlement — is a self-describing 128-bit GYST UUIDv8. Type, namespace, time, fractal lineage, a 16-bit probability signal, and provenance live in the bits, not in a lookup table. Routing, filtering, and roll-up become O(1) bitmask operations; the JSON dies at the exchange edge and everything downstream is wirespeed.

3The spawn tree — the ledger audits itself

market 0x3B0 depth0 (root: KXMLB-26-LAD) └─ order 0x3A4 depth1 sig=0.4100 client_order_id = 1e14743d7d7 ├─ ack 0x3A6 depth2 sig=0.4090 (= avg fill price, from exchange) └─ fill 0x3A7 depth2 sig=0.4100 (exchange fill_id seeded) └─ settle 0x3A9 depth1 sig=1.0000 (result)

Every fill/settlement is a child UUID (fractal_depth+1, generation+1, namespace=fnv1a12(parent)). P&L is a walk of the tree — no joins, no orphaned rows possible. A stream consumer can reconstruct any position from the UUIDs alone.
