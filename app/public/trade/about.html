1The substrate — what a bet IS

Every market event is a self-describing 128-bit UUID. Type, namespace, time, fractal lineage, a 16-bit probability signal, and provenance live in the bits. Routing, filtering, and reconciliation become O(1) bitmask operations — the JSON dies at the exchange edge and everything downstream is wirespeed.

2The machines — daemons, all supervised, all zero-token

btc_trend + trend_engine x4 — 15-min trend threads for BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP/DOGE. Reads spot momentum from our own UUID tape, enters trend side early-window, exits +15¢/−10¢.
profit_scalp + maker — multi-series scalp + passive limit-quote engine across 5 crypto 15M series.
mission_control — the gate. Every order flows through it: caps, kill switch, floor guard, passkey, ledger minting.
crossvenue_engine + poly_executor — Kalshi vs Polymarket arb scanner +( dormant until wallet funded).
uuid_ingest + tick_service + kalshi_ws — the tape. Spot + book ticks minted as UUIDs, live WebSocket feed, normalized price stream.
fill_poller + oracle + evidence_engine — truth sync. Exchange fills → positions, settlement verification, statistical edge validation per lane.
supervisor + governor + recovery_engine — the sanity loop. Singleton locks, heartbeats, hung-daemon restarts, risk limits, crash capture + auto-restart.
x_watcher + news_supply_engine + shadow_index + uptick_spiral — signal layer: X/Twitter sentiment, RSS news, whale tracking, forecast accuracy scoring (Brier).
dry_run x4 + sweep_watch + calendar + funding_feed — paper engine ($1/$3 clip comparison), cash sweep alerts, settlement calendar, wallet funding watcher.
