00WHAT THIS IS

TIME is a magazine that reads the news, calls the future, and puts money where its mouth is. Every story on the front page is a live object: it has a routable identity, a forecast attached to it, a market it maps to, and a bet button that actually fires. Not a mockup of the loop — the loop itself, in public.

01THE NORTH STAR — EVERYTHING IS A UUIDv8

One addressing scheme for the whole machine. Every market, every order, every article, every forecast, every vote is a routable, bettable, transactional UUIDv8 object. Not a row id, not a slug — a 128-bit GYST UUIDv8 whose bits carry what the object is, when it was born, and what it believed.

Because identity is content-addressed and signal-bearing, the graph is traversable in both directions: from a story to every bet it spawned, and from any bet back to the exact article, forecast, and market state that justified it. Identity is the audit trail.

02THE LOOP

news→ forecasts→ markets→ bets→ evidence

The news supply engine sweeps the feeds and mints each AI story as a UUIDv8 article — that is the front page. The fleet reads the same wire and publishes OUR CALLS: live forecasts with probabilities. Each story's topic maps onto open Kalshi markets; the tradable card on every article lets a reader size a position and FIRE — the order is minted as a child of the story's UUID and routed to the exchange. Fills, rejects, and P&L flow back into the evidence engine, which scores every call the fleet has ever made. The magazine doesn't just report the future — it is graded on it.

04THE HONESTY MACHINERY

EVIDENCE ENGINE

Every forecast the fleet publishes is kept. When the outcome lands, the call is scored — calibration, Brier, hit rate — and the scorecard is served back to the same API that published the call. OUR CALLS is not a boast board; it is a ledger of record.

LOSS PUBLISHING

Losses are published with the same prominence as wins. Rejected orders stay in the ledger with status rejected; losing positions stay on the equity curve. A system that hides its losses is marketing. This one ships its receipts.

  • Caps are real: guest orders are PIN-gated, $2 per order, $20 per day, ≤4 contracts — enforced server-side, not in the UI.
  • Kill switch: one flag halts every order lane; the UI would rather show a rejection than a lie.
  • Shadow twins: every strategy runs a paper double, so live-vs-expected divergence is itself evidence.

05THE SAYS.COM NETWORK VISION

TIME is one node of a network. The same substrate — UUIDv8 identity, the loop, the fleet, the honesty machinery — generalizes into the says.com network: a constellation of city and topic portals where every claim is an object, every object can carry a market, and every market resolves into public evidence. A city says a transit line opens in spring; the network mints the claim, prices it, and grades it. An AI magazine says chips get cheaper; same machinery. Journalism where every sentence that predicts something is a position you can inspect, bet against, and score. That's the network. TIME is where it learned to talk.
