How fees work — the grind tax

The trap: Kalshi charges a fee per contract, per fill — roughly $0.007–$0.01 fixed + 10% of any winnings. It does NOT scale down with your bet size. So a $0.01 micro-clip pays the same ~$0.007 fee as analysis allows, meaning fees can be 50–150% of your trade before any price edge. We lost $48 to fees on $34 of volume (143% drag).

The rule we now enforce: never trade a clip where edge < fee. Minimum clip = $1.00. At $1.00 the same ~$0.01 fee is only ~1% of the trade — survivable. Below ~$0.50/clip the math can never win.

Sweet spot (this account): $1–$3 per clip, 15-min crypto momentum only, edge>fee gate on every entry. 10% of bankroll per position, hard $25 cash backstop (never spend the last $25).
