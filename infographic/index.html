The Thesis — What This Actually Is

Every entity — a prediction market, a price tick, a trade, a city post, a vendor, a wallet, an agent contract — is a self-describing 128-bit UUIDv8. Type, namespace, time, fractal lineage, a 16-bit probability signal, and provenance live in the bits, not in a lookup table.

Consequence: routing, filtering, and roll-up become O(1) bitmask operations — 4 byte-shifts instead of a DB join + JSON parse. An agent reads a feed of raw 128-bit integers and knows what everything is at wirespeed. That is the entire moat: the address IS the data model.

The internet already speaks UUID. This bends every foreign system — Polymarket, Kalshi, OSM, X, a farmers market in Sonoma — onto one mineable signal substrate.
